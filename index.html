The umbrella hour

A light drizzle of forgotten umbrellas, clearing when someone remembers your name.

Barometer
Falling, then remembering
Humidity
Two umbrellas’ worth

Leave a little room by the door.

The seven-day outlook

    Recorded with care. Accuracy remains a matter of atmosphere.

    For contemplation, not navigation.
